# Usability test

## 10a. Test with a real user

**Tester:** Tester 1, a 4th-year student at MSU-IIT (matches the Gon persona).
**How we tested:** In discord through voice call and sharescreen. They gave permission for me to take notes. I do not include their name or face.

### Task 1: Get in line for Enrollment / Change of Subjects

| Question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Did they finish it? | Yes |
| About how long? | 47 seconds |
| Where they hesitated | He did not hesitate |
| What they said | Nothing, he just fill in his name and id number and what he needed to get in virtual line in registrar. |

### Task 2: How many people are ahead? What is your number?

| Question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Did they finish it? | Yes |
| About how long? | 33 seconds |
| Where they hesitated | He did not hesitate |
| What they said | He noticed immediately the changes in their place (My place) and he saw immediately his ticket number and the persons ahead of him. |

### Task 3: As staff, call the next person and mark them as served

| Question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Did they finish it? | Yes |
| About how long? | 94 seconds |
| Where they hesitated | He did not hesitate |
| What they said | He can immediately see the students virtually in line and also he can immediately calls them and mark as served and no show. |

### Which persona rules held up

| Rule | Held up? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gon 1: Because Gon wants to avoid long physical lines, the interface must make joining a queue easy. | ✅ | Tester 1 could easily operate the app without asking |
| Gon 2: Because Gon needs to know their position, the app must display real-time queue status clearly. | ✅ | Tester 1 did not mention it but he easily knows the real-time queue status clearly. |
| Gon 3: Because Gon may be in class, the app must provide timely notifications. | ❌ (not fully) | The app shows a "Called" banner on the page, but it has no push notification yet. |
| Killua 1: Because Killua needs to manage students quickly, the dashboard must show the active queue clearly. | ✅ | The dashboard shows the active queue clearly and also records history. |
| Killua 2: Because Killua must call students in order, the app must provide a simple Call Next button. | ✅ | He don't need to worry about the order he can just click the CALL button and the order is still accurate. |
| Killua 3: Because Killua needs to track completed requests, the app must allow ticket statuses to be updated easily. | ✅ | The dashboard shows the active queue clearly and also records history. Also it update status easily and quickly. |

## 10b. One improvement

**Problem:** After joining, the green message says “Your ticket is #4”, but the page shows R-004. A user can think those are two different numbers

**Why I picked it:** I choose this because the user might confuse what "#4" ticket number means compare to "R-004" but they are actually the same.

**Fix:** I make the green message says "Your ticket is R-004 instead of "#4"" for consistency and to prevent any confusions.
